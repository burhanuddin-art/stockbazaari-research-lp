What is Stockbazaari?

Stockbazaari is a SEBI-registered Research Analyst (Reg. No. INH000018665). We publish research on Nifty, Bank Nifty and Sensex options and selected stocks — key levels, research calls and the reasoning behind them.

Do you guarantee profits or fixed returns?

No. No research analyst can guarantee returns, and SEBI does not allow such claims. Each research call gives you a defined entry, stop-loss and rationale so you can plan your trade — the decision and the risk remain yours.

How will I receive research calls?

Our research desk will explain the delivery channels and plan details on your call.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can start with a free trial of our research calls. After the trial, our research services are paid subscriptions — our team explains the plans, fees and refund policy before you decide. There is no obligation to subscribe.

Will you ask for my trading login or OTP?

Never. We do not execute trades, manage funds or ask for login IDs, passwords or OTPs. Pay only to Stockbazaari's official account — never to personal accounts or UPI IDs.

How do I raise a complaint?

Email info@stockbazaari.com. If your complaint is not resolved, you can escalate it on SEBI SCORES (scores.sebi.gov.in) or the SMART ODR portal (smartodr.in).
